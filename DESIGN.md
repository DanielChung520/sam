# SAM Mobile - Design System

## Product Identity
SAM (Sales Assistant Manager) - A privacy-first sales CRM that combines LINE-style messaging with intelligent contact management. The physical metaphor: a **leather-bound executive organizer** meets **encrypted vault** - warm, tactile, trustworthy, with an undercurrent of high-tech security.

## Visual Strategy
- Photography: Professional headshots, warm office environments
- Graphics: Minimal line icons in emerald tones, score badges with fire/seed/sleep metaphors
- USB vault concept visualized as a subtle shield/lock motif

## Typography
- Titles: 28px/800 weight
- Section headers: 18px/700
- Card titles: 16px/700
- Body: 14px/400
- Labels: 13px/600
- Tab labels: 10px/600

## Key Design Decisions
- Neumorphic soft card style (dual shadow) for all cards
- LINE-inspired chat bubbles with emerald sent / white received
- Score badges as pill tags with emoji + gradient
- USB status as a colored badge at top of every screen
- Bottom tab bar with rounded top corners and upward shadow
- No borders - depth through shadow only

## Design Don'ts
- No white card backgrounds (use #F0F2F5)
- No border-based separation
- No rectangular selection indicators
- No cold blue color schemes
- No generic tech aesthetics
